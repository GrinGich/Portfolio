Game Project 4:
Bat out of hell

Genre: FPS, Speed Shooter

Engine: Unity

Platform: PC

Team Size: 16

Time: 9 weeks


The Final game project from PlaygroundSquad.
Bat out of hell is a fast paced speed Shooter where your goal is to get to the end before the bomb on your head blows up.

Developed at PlaygroundSquad by 16 students in 9 weeks.
