Game Project 3:
Frightline

Genre: Horror, Puzzle

Engine: Unity

Platform: PC

Team Size: 16

Time: 9 weeks


FrightLine was the third game project at PlaygroundSquad. The Game is atmospheric puzzle "horror" game where the player has to utilise their doll form but also interact through the spirit world.

  • Plug and play the design philosophy behind the menus and espacially the buttons, is that all the button functions we need are available in my "MasterButtonScript" where you select a button and the relevant variables need to that button are shown.


  • Ease of Use from the buttons made it easy to iterate on different styles of menus requested by the designers.
